Professional Background

Graduated summa cum laude with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (University of Advancing Technology, 2017), I combine rigorous academic training with six years of real-world investigative experience as an Alaska Department of Public Safety Investigator and five years in IT support at Geek Squad. This uncommon trajectory has shaped my approach to technology: attention to detail, crisis management under pressure, and understanding both the technical AND human sides of systems.

I hold Texas Journeyman Electrician Apprentice certification and have recently focused on software development, robotics, and industrial automation. My portfolio includes security-hardened web applications, database-driven tools, and PLC ladder logic projects demonstrating my commitment to bridging physical and digital infrastructure.

Technology & Nature

Technological Philosophy

Humanity's greatest responsibility is one of progressing our technological power with balance. As we continue to grow, we must take care to utilize our intelligence and innovation in ways that are conscious of their effects on the environments we serve—whether that environment is a manufacturing floor, a healthcare system, or a community network.

I believe in personal accountability through code quality, systematic testing, and security-first design principles. Just as "fiat justitia ruat caelum"—let justice be done though the heavens fall—I approach development with unwavering standards for correctness, even when it demands difficult refactoring or extended debugging time.
