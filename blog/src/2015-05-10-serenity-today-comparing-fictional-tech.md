The technology in the film Serenity is not very far off from what we can see today. We are at the verge of breakthrough on all fronts of technology that will change the way we interact, travel, and communicate. The ideas present in the film are simply the evolutionary grandfather to what we see in new innovations today. As I lay out the research in this paper, I hope that you too will see how in this case science fiction could not be fictitious at all and therefore the responsibility of how we use technology lies with us.

Serenity leaves us with a feeling of hope and wonder for technology. The devices and systems in its two hour span are just rudimentary enough to leave us believing that they are the possible next generation of many technologies available to us today. After much research and thought, I have come to the conclusion that this may be more than just a feeling. It, in fact, may just be an accurate foretelling of where things are to go.

One such technology is communications by way of holographic projections and recordings. In the film, a message is played at a ruined city describing the aftermath of what happened there. This message takes the form of a three dimensional image beamed above the surface of a device using light to represent form, movement, and color. There are statistics and findings on three screen like overlays being projected in addition to the person, showing the findings as the person is talking about them. Presentations like these may not be as far fetched as you may think though. Emile Venere (2013) has this to say about the technology we have present today, “Researchers have created tiny holograms using a "metasurface" capable of the ultra-efficient control of light, representing a potential new technology for advanced sensors, high-resolution displays and information processing” (para. 1). This is astoundingly similar in potential to the description of the technology in the film. Using light to project an image off a device or layer had been proven. Now all that is left is to scale the result up to the level portrayed in Serenity and many other science fiction media.

Holographic technology is not the only technology we are close to though. Serenity features a few devices we already have in production today. Take for instance the scene where the crew is checking the planets for a safe route using a shipboard computer. The program or application running on this computer looks to map star and anomalies in space and provide information on each at a glance. This system is the same function that we currently use GoogleMaps and other navigation software for. Google is especially of note in this endeavor because of their work on Google Sky Map, an application for mobile and tablet devices that allows you to look in the sky and identify satellites, planets, stars, and other space objects from earth by simply pointing your device into the air at the place you want to look at. Admittedly, this is a small scope to the vast universe of planets pictures in the film, but it shows a mastery of navigation even in our relatively small space exploration endeavors. This comparison highlights the idea that we may one day have similar technology and be able to pull up information and pictures of distant planets much the same way we do for cities, roads, and our favorite fast food diner now.

The technology of space navigation brings up another great point of interest featured in the movie; the advent of public and private space travel. Serenity features space travel as commonplace as car and bus travel is today. Ships go from planet to planet and whole industries are devoted to their travels. In comparison we are pretty new to space travel in general, having achieved humans in space relatively recently (1961). In the current climate space remains a wonder for many and as that wonder grows companies seek ways to deliver on the untapped market. Companies such as Virgin Galactic and XCOR Aerospace already offer the ability to travel just outside the border of the atmosphere to anyone who can pay their price. There are also private companies like SpaceX (2013) who states they, “design, manufacture and launch advanced rockets and spacecraft. The company was founded in 2002 to revolutionize space technology, with the ultimate goal of enabling people to live on other planets”. Companies such as these compete for contracts with NASA and other agencies in order to provide support and equipment to tackle the varied needs space travel requires. Through the research, testing and eventual implementation of these innovative theories, companies are paving the way for more commonplace space travel.

The technology in Serenity does not just show the positive progress and good that comes from innovator’s progress though. It outlines the bad as well. Subliminal messaging, a marketing and psychological practice that has been experimented with since the 1950’s is used against one of the main characters named River to unlock subconscious a become a violent killing machine. Such tactics have been in use to debatable degrees of success in current times. Philip Merikle (n.d) states that, “Over the years there have been literally hundreds of studies"..."these studies show that considerable information capable of informing decisions and guiding actions is perceived even when observers do not experience any awareness of perceiving" (para. 1). Should this research prove true and technology advance to the stage that these messages were powerful enough to make a noticeable impact, the level of control and morality of that control on the subjects receiving the messages could be just as catastrophic as River’s episode in the film.

Another example that fits this theme of balancing technologies evils with its good are the mood altering drugs that are dispersed upon the population of the planet River once stayed in. These drugs worked for their intended purpose of reducing violence and instilling peace and order passively. Their effect was noticed immediately, but as time went on the long term consequences became more apparent as people began to give up their work, families, and eventually their lives out of disinterest. The adverse effects on a small segment of the population was then covered up by parliament and thus created the destruction caused by the entity known as the Reavers. This kind of thing is all too well known in today’s medical industry climate where drugs are often pushed passed development without the proper testing causing side-effects in their users that no one wants to take responsibility for.

As we can see, the technology presented in films like Serenity is nearing the realm of reality as technology continues to find solutions to the problems standing in the way of our eventual progress. There will always be stumbling blocks set to hinder our progress with innovation, but the innovators of our time do as they have always done. From the safety razor to space travel, the will and determination of these great men and woman show time and time again that it will triumph over the obstacles set before it. Society will eventually fall in line with the innovations and such progresses will breed new more efficient results as they cycle continues. What we will see today will almost certainly be a better version tomorrow until eventually we realize the fantasy of science fiction media today is not so crazy as it once seemed. It should not all be about forward momentum and progress though. In order to avoid instances showcased in the film, like River’s sub conscious killing spree or the accidental destruction of an entire city, we must make it a priority to police ourselves on developments and take responsibility for our actions and the effects our innovation will have lest we create a monster like the Reavers to restore order to the balance and enforce the responsibility itself.
