What can Cyrus Field's example teach us about innovation today? Cyrus Field is quite an inspiration when it comes to the sheer dedication required to succeed. His perseverance in the face of an inordinate amounts of failure and his sheer will to see his dream become a reality highlights what it takes to succeed in innovation. Though many things stood in his way, he overcame them with confidence, perseverance, and hard work. These are all characteristics that even the least wealthy man can attain, given they are as dedicated and willing as Field was.

>"There will always be a certain amount of risk in any endeavor, but determination to succeed, ability to stay positive in the face of tough challenges, and learning from mistakes and failures provide the greatest chance of traversing that chasm of uncertainty."

Even though Field was quite wealthy, his wealth could not make the many attempts he made any easier. He had to take his failure in stride and raise money for attempt after attempt with little more than an idea and the drive and determination to see it succeed. In my opinion, this speaks volumes to what it takes to develop any great idea into a reality. There will always be a certain amount of risk in any endeavor, but determination to succeed, ability to stay positive in the face of tough challenges, and learning from mistakes and failures provide the greatest chance of traversing that chasm of uncertainty.

Any idea, invention, innovation, or project will always come with a degree of uncertainty. It is up to the innovator, the inventor, the project leader to plan for this failure and overcome the obstacles with renewed fervor each time. Nothing great can be accomplished without great effort. To succeed you must put in everything with the risk of receiving nothing in return, all the while expecting beyond all reason to come out on top. I think that looking to the great innovators of the 20th century, we can see that no amount of time and innovation has lessened the sheer determination it takes to build something remarkable.
