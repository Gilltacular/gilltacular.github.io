Technology is consolidating itself. We started with many diverse fields of study that took specialized teams to understand and modify. Today we are bottle-necked by the advancement of the technology itself. Solar energy, medical advancement, nano-machines, self driving cars, and extended space travel are all things we can envision, but need much more proper testing and development to implement in practical ways.

The innovations of the future will be hybrids endeavors; a mix of past innovations combined in new ways. Nano-machines for example, a tiny molecule sized simple machine that can work on a level we cannot even see with the naked eye. The implications of such devices in the medical profession would be incredibly revolutionary. Another example is solar energy. wWat if we expanded on this idea of natural energy and were able to devise energy gained by nano-machine ecosystems in much the same way that plants and animals do, these machines could consume something to generate energy and expel the needed nutrients for other machines to consume and run their process.

No matter where we look, the innovations of the future are bleeding through previous boundaries and conceptions. In my eyes the future of innovation is one where the climate changes so drastically that the way we understood and did things before will seem foreign and simple in comparison. We will shift toward utilizing more of the natural processes in life and less on the global consumption of resources to depletion. There will be automated processes for keeping us healthy and happy aiding our brain and body alike in their productions of the chemicals that drive our emotions and functions. The overall acceptance of technology on a whole will go through the roof as it becomes even more commonplace in life than it is today and honestly I feel we will be better for it.
